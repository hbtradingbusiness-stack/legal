Apple and Google Handle Payments and Refunds

PREZLO Pro Subscriptions are purchased through the Application Store, being Apple In-App Purchase on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android. Apple or Google, as applicable, is the merchant of record for those purchases, and processes payments, renewals, cancellations, taxes, payment methods, and refund requests according to its own terms and policies. PREZLO does not directly charge your payment card, does not store your full payment-card information, and does not directly issue refunds for purchases made through the Application Store. Refund eligibility and refund approval are determined by Apple or Google, not by PREZLO.

How to Request a Refund

Apple (iPhone, iPad): Go to https://reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, choose “Request a refund,” select the reason for the request, choose the PREZLO subscription or purchase, and submit the request.

Google Play (Android): Go to https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions or use Google Play support, sign in with the Google Account used for the purchase, find the PREZLO subscription, and request a refund. Google Play refund requests may also be made from the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.

Apple or Google, not PREZLO, determines whether a refund is approved. If a refund is approved, the timing of funds returning to your payment method is handled by that store and your payment provider.

Free Trial

PREZLO offers a Free Trial of 7 days on its Pro Subscription plans. The Free Trial is an introductory offer administered by the Application Store as part of a subscription purchase, and requires an Apple Account or Google Account with a valid payment method. Free Trial eligibility is determined by the Application Store, not by PREZLO.

During the Free Trial period itself, no charge is made and therefore no refund is applicable. However, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the Free Trial ends, the trial converts automatically into a paid Pro Subscription and you will be charged. You are responsible for cancelling through your Apple Account or Google Play subscription settings before the trial ends if you do not wish to be charged. Once a charge has been made, any refund request must be submitted to Apple or Google as described above.

Cancellation Is Not the Same as a Refund

Subscription cancellation is managed through your Apple Account or Google Play subscription settings. You are responsible for canceling before the next renewal date if you do not want your subscription to renew. Refund requests, if any, must be submitted to Apple or Google through that store’s refund process. Upon cancellation, your access to real-time features ends at the end of the period already paid for.
