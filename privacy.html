3. Necessary information and choices

You are not generally legally obliged to provide information to PREZLO. Some technical, access and transaction information is needed to provide the app, recognise a subscription, protect the Service or respond to you. Without it, the corresponding function may not work. Sending a support message or submitting content is voluntary.

Device settings control notification permission. In-app preferences control supported notification categories when you can access those settings. Refusing notifications does not itself cancel paid access. A device advertising setting may limit certain identifiers but does not necessarily stop every analytics or server-side event.

Optional analytics and advertising measurement do not start in the app before the age group is determined and are not enabled for users under 18. For adults, we obtain permission before processing that requires consent under applicable law or platform rules, and honour refusal and withdrawal in server-side disclosures as well. Accepting the Terms is not consent to tracking. Contact legal@getprezlo.com to exercise privacy choices; email does not replace a particular opt-out control or preference signal required by law.

We do not use these data to tailor securities recommendations or make automated decisions about credit, employment, insurance or similar eligibility.

5. Advertising, measurement and websites

For adults, subject to required permissions, Meta receives app and subscription events, including through a server-to-server integration. Its app-scoped identifier is not your name, but may support association with other information held by Meta. Applicable age restrictions and privacy choices are also applied to the server integration; these routes do not send events for users identified as under 18.

Earlier attribution may associate a link with an installation or subscription. The Detour app attribution component is disabled in this version; we do not read the clipboard for attribution or derive a fingerprint from device characteristics for identification or attribution. Earlier attribution is not itself proof of your identity or entitlement to a benefit.

We do not trade personal information for cash as a separate data product. Certain disclosures for advertising or attribution may nevertheless be “sale”, “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under a law's definitions. Where that law applies, the corresponding opt-out rights apply. We do not sell personal information of users under 18, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising or use it for targeted advertising.

The getprezlo.com redirect site uses aggregate click measurements rather than a PREZLO advertising pixel. Hosting and security providers still process request metadata. Other linked sites may collect their own data. We do not currently implement a response to browser Do Not Track signals; this is distinct from legally recognised opt-out preference signals, which we will honour where required by applicable law. Contact legal@getprezlo.com for privacy requests.

We do not enable session replay. Any introduction of recording or materially different collection requires updated disclosures and permissions where required.

7. Retention

Retention depends on purpose, record type, legal requirements and the need to address transactions, security or disputes. We do not promise immediate deletion on uninstall or one universal retention period.

Active and historical records are not presently subject to a single automated expiry rule. Backups rotate by number of copies, currently the newest 30, rather than a guaranteed number of days. This can leave a copy for longer than 30 days if backup creation stops.

Our retention decisions must be limited to justified purposes. Where a request is granted, we will remove or restrict the applicable active records and address relevant provider copies. Information that must lawfully remain will be restricted to that purpose. Residual backups will not be used to restore deleted information to ordinary service use without reapplying the relevant deletion/restriction.

9. Deletion, billing and children

Uninstalling the app does not automatically erase server records or cancel a subscription. A privacy request is not automatically a store cancellation. If you want both, say so and use the cancellation guide; we will help route the requests.

The Service is not offered to children under 13. Before access, we determine an age group using limited information; a store rating is not age verification. Before an age group is determined and for a person identified as under 13, processing is limited to what is permitted and necessary for the check, blocking access, security or responding to a request, not tracking or marketing. If we learn that information was collected from a child under 13, we stop impermissible use and address deletion with our providers, except for limited retention permitted by law.

Users aged 13–17 receive the Service with limited operational information. We do not enable Meta, Mixpanel, PostHog or Detour attribution for them or send their subscription events to Meta. Transaction records, delivery of requested alerts, submitted content, necessary error diagnostics, support and security continue only to the extent necessary for their purpose. Parental subscription approval does not authorise optional measurement. We do not send them messages encouraging purchases or a return to the app; necessary service notices and requested alerts within an active service are not consent to marketing messages.

When an existing user is identified as a minor, we stop optional processing and also review previously collected information. Information without a basis for continued processing will be deleted or restricted, and we will contact appropriate providers and recipients within applicable deadlines; we do not necessarily wait for a deletion request. Backups will not return that information to ordinary use without reapplying the restriction.

Minors, parents and guardians may contact legal@getprezlo.com, including to correct age information or withdraw approval. We verify authority proportionately and respect the minor’s independent statutory rights. A government ID is not required by default. Growing older does not automatically activate advertising permission or replace any required consent.
