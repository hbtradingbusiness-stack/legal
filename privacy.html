Apple or Google handles payment credentials. PREZLO does not receive your complete payment-card number through the store purchase flow. An IP address can be used by an analytics provider to infer an approximate location; this is different from GPS access.

Please do not send passwords, full card numbers, brokerage credentials or identity documents unless a specific, justified request and appropriate secure channel are provided. Comments submitted to a public feature can be visible to other users. Sharing through another app sends the content you choose to that destination and its recipients.

Friend Gift. When a friend taps “Claim my gift” on the gift web page, we record that the gift was opened and keep a salted hash of the request's IP address. We use that hash only to recognise the same network when the app first opens within 20 minutes. The app then asks for iOS permission to paste the copied gift link from the clipboard, to confirm the gift; iOS shows its own paste prompt, and you can decline. The sender's first name is shown to the friend who opens the gift. Because a free month depends on a friend's first payment, an earned month can show the sender that a friend who claimed the gift has paid. We may send the sender a push notification when a free month is earned. Friend Gift events sent to our analytics provider are linked to your app-user identifier and include the gift identifier, how the gift was claimed, the sharing channel and the environment, but not the sender's first name. Trials started with a Friend Gift are not reported to Meta. The programme terms are in the Friend Gift Terms. The Friend Gift programme is for users aged 18 and over only, and its push notification is not intended for anyone under 18.

3. Necessary information and choices

You are not generally legally obliged to provide information to PREZLO. Some technical, access and transaction information is needed to provide the app, recognise a subscription, protect the Service or respond to you. Without it, the corresponding function may not work. Sending a support message or submitting content is voluntary.

Device settings control notification permission. In-app preferences control supported notification categories when you can access those settings. Refusing notifications does not itself cancel paid access. A device advertising setting may limit certain identifiers but does not necessarily stop every analytics or server-side event.

The app does not currently show a tracking-permission prompt. On iOS, without that permission the device advertising identifier is not available to us or to Meta; on Android, the Meta SDK in our app is configured not to collect the advertising identifier. Where applicable law requires consent for particular processing, we will obtain it before that processing, and honour refusal and withdrawal in server-side disclosures as well. Accepting the Terms is not consent to tracking. Contact legal@getprezlo.com to exercise privacy choices; email does not replace a particular opt-out control or preference signal required by law.

We do not use these data to tailor securities recommendations or make automated decisions about credit, employment, insurance or similar eligibility.

5. Advertising, measurement and websites

Meta receives app, trial and purchase events through its SDK in the app and through a server-to-server integration, for all users. Its app-scoped identifier is not your name, but may support association with other information held by Meta. Privacy choices that apply to this transfer are also applied to the server integration.

Earlier attribution may associate a link with an installation or subscription. App versions before 1.2.5 included Detour's install-attribution component: on first launch it could read the clipboard once on iOS and send device characteristics (such as device model, operating system, screen, language and time zone) and IP address to match an installation with an earlier link click. From version 1.2.5 the app no longer includes this component. We do not read the clipboard for attribution, except that the Friend Gift flow described in section 2 reads a copied gift link, with your iOS paste permission, to confirm a gift. We do not derive a device fingerprint for attribution. Earlier attribution is not itself proof of your identity or entitlement to a benefit.

Partner offers. If you tap the partner offer in the app, you leave the app for the brokerage firm's website, currently Colmex Pro Ltd, which collects and processes the information you give it under its own privacy policy. The link carries only our partner reference. We do not send the brokerage firm your name, contact details, device identifiers or app activity. Our analytics provider records, linked to your app-user identifier, that the offer screen was opened and that its button was tapped, and from where in the app, to measure the offer. To calculate and verify our commission, the firm may provide us with limited information about accounts opened through our link, such as an account reference, relevant dates and the deposit and activity information needed to calculate the commission. We use it only for that purpose and for our accounting and legal obligations.

We do not trade personal information for cash as a separate data product. Certain disclosures for advertising or attribution may nevertheless be “sale”, “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under a law's definitions. Where that law applies, the corresponding opt-out rights apply. We do not ask users for their age. App usage, trial and purchase events are sent to Meta and to our analytics providers for measurement and improvement for all users, including users aged 13–17.

The getprezlo.com redirect site uses aggregate click measurements rather than a PREZLO advertising pixel. Friend Gift pages on getprezlo.com record that a gift was opened and keep a salted IP hash, as described in section 2. Hosting and security providers still process request metadata. Other linked sites may collect their own data. We do not currently implement a response to browser Do Not Track signals; this is distinct from legally recognised opt-out preference signals, which we will honour where required by applicable law. Contact legal@getprezlo.com for privacy requests.

We do not enable session replay. Any introduction of recording or materially different collection requires updated disclosures and permissions where required.

9. Deletion, billing and children

Uninstalling the app does not automatically erase server records or cancel a subscription. A privacy request is not automatically a store cancellation. If you want both, say so and use the cancellation guide; we will help route the requests.

The Service is not offered to children under 13. We do not currently run an age check, and a store rating is not age verification. If we learn that information was collected from a child under 13, we stop impermissible use and address deletion with our providers, except for limited retention permitted by law.

Users aged 13–17 may use the Service under the Terms' rules for minors. Because we do not currently verify age, the processing described in this Policy, including analytics and advertising measurement subject to required permissions, may apply to them as it does to other users.

A minor, parent or guardian may ask us to stop optional processing of a minor's information, or to delete or restrict it. We will handle the request, including with relevant providers and recipients, within applicable deadlines. Backups will not return that information to ordinary use without reapplying the restriction.

Contact legal@getprezlo.com for these requests. We verify authority proportionately and respect the minor’s independent statutory rights. A government ID is not required by default.
